### What we build

Nalar is the AI operating layer for enterprise agents. It connects a company's apps (SAP, Odoo, Salesforce, Google Sheets, WhatsApp and 100+ more) into one company graph, then puts agents to work on top of it: sales, collections, accounting, inventory, procurement, payroll and more.

- **Runs where your data lives.** Your cloud, inside your VPC, or on-prem. Bring any model, including open weights on your own servers.
- **Agents ask before they act.** They inherit the roles people already have. Anything that changes a record, moves money or sends a message waits for a named person.
- **Every action is logged.** The audit trail stays in your environment and belongs to you.
- **Speaks the business.** Answers in English or Bahasa Indonesia, built around Indonesia's UU PDP.

### Who it's for

Enterprises in Indonesia across FMCG, banking, retail, telco, plantation and mining. Nalar is in closed beta and onboards a few companies at a time.
